# L5 — Finances

## Purpose

Creates the financial freedom that removes constraint from all other pillars. L5 depends on L3 (primary income) and benefits from consistent savings discipline. The 2026 *intent* is stability and positive cash flow rather than aggressive wealth generation — a defensible posture while the L3 Driver (career promotion) is the primary focus. **Corrected Jan–Aug 2026 data (2026-08-16) shows YTD cash flow is net-positive (+$3,217.57 through Aug 16), though 5 of the first 7 months individually closed negative** — see Strategic Context below.

**Core principle (L5):** Build the financial floor before reaching for the ceiling.
