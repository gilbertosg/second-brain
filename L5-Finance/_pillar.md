# L5 — Finances

## Purpose

Creates the financial freedom that removes constraint from all other pillars. L5 depends on L3 (primary income) and benefits from consistent savings discipline. The 2026 focus is stability and positive cash flow rather than aggressive wealth generation — a defensible posture while the L3 Driver (career promotion) is the primary focus.

**Core principle (L5):** Build the financial floor before reaching for the ceiling.

---

## Strategic Context

L5 in 2026 operates as a **maintenance and growth** layer. The couple finance KRs (savings rate, positive cash flow, MPI growth) form a compounding wealth foundation. The parents' support and insurance KRs represent a fixed monthly commitment — a non-negotiable baseline that must be factored into all budget calculations.

**New home decision (L2 KR)** is the highest-risk L5 event in 2026. A real estate purchase or new lease will significantly alter cash flow, savings capacity, and potentially MPI. The bi-weekly Dream & Finance meetings with Luisa are the primary governance mechanism for this decision.

**MPI (Monthly Passive Income)** at 10%/month growth rate is an aggressive compounding target. Track the starting baseline to make this measurable.

## Key Methodologies Applied

- **Tiago Forte (PARA):** Finance files in Google Drive `02 Areas/Finance` (budgets, taxes). Organized for instant retrieval during Dream & Finance meetings.
- **Cal Newport (Slow Productivity):** Prioritize fewer, high-quality financial moves over frequent micro-optimization.
