# L5 — Finances

## Purpose

Creates the financial freedom that removes constraint from all other pillars. L5 depends on L3 (primary income) and benefits from consistent savings discipline. The 2026 *intent* is stability and positive cash flow rather than aggressive wealth generation — a defensible posture while the L3 Driver (career promotion) is the primary focus. **Actual Jan–Jul 2026 data shows this intent is not yet the reality** — see Strategic Context below.

**Core principle (L5):** Build the financial floor before reaching for the ceiling.

L5 in 2026 operates as a **maintenance and growth** layer. The couple finance KRs (savings rate, positive cash flow, MPI growth) form a compounding wealth foundation. The parents' support and insurance KRs represent a fixed monthly commitment — a non-negotiable baseline that must be factored into all budget calculations.

First *recurring* mortgage payment is scheduled 2026-08-01 ($3,200). The household moved out of the previous CIRRUS apartment 2026-06-08. This is no longer a pending decision — see [[L5-Finance/household-income-2026]], [[L5-Finance/transaction-analysis-2026-jan-jul.md]], and [[02-Indexes/topic_maps/household-finance-relationship-map]] for the resolved status and its L2 implications.
