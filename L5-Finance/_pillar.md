# L5 — Finances

## Purpose

Creates the financial freedom that removes constraint from all other pillars. L5 depends on L3 (primary income) and benefits from consistent savings discipline. The 2026 *intent* is stability and positive cash flow rather than aggressive wealth generation — a defensible posture while the L3 Driver (career promotion) is the primary focus. **The refreshed Jan–Oct 2026 ledger (2026-10-24) shows headline YTD net of +$37,575.18 through Oct 23, but +$29,331.20 of that is a one-time loan inflow. Adjusted YTD net is +$8,243.98, and 6 of the 9 complete months individually closed negative** — see Strategic Context below.

**Core principle (L5):** Build the financial floor before reaching for the ceiling.
