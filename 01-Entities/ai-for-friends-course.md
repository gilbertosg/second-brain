# AI for Friends (Course)

**Type:** Initiative — a self-created AI literacy course.
**Creator/Instructor:** Gilberto Silva Gonzalez.
**Audience:** Friends, in professional roles spanning HR, industrial engineering, legal, and marketing.
**Status:** Active — Modules 1–3 ingested 2026-08-02; Module 4 planned but not yet authored.

## What This Is

A 3-module (soon 4) hands-on AI-fluency curriculum: Module 1 teaches AI/prompting fundamentals conceptually, Module 2 is an optional computer/terminal warm-up, and Module 3 walks through installing and using Claude Code as a document-automation assistant. No programming background assumed. Full lesson-level synthesis lives in [[L4-Growth/course-ai-for-friends]] and its three module-notes files.

## Why This Exists as an Entity

This is the first page in `01-Entities` — established here because the course is a recurring connector across three pillars rather than content that belongs to any single one:

- **L4-Growth** — the course itself is a learning/teaching artifact and counts toward the "4 AI/side projects" 2026 OKR.
- **L3-Career** — creating and delivering the course is concrete evidence for the Agent Award KR and the Principal-promotion AI-differentiator narrative; Module 3's confidentiality guidance is a direct personal action item for Blue Origin work.
- **L7-Contribute** — a realized instance of the previously-unwired "L4 → L7 Teaching Loop," and a ready-made source for blog/video content toward the publishing KRs.
