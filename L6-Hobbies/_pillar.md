# L6 — Hobbies & Celebrate Life

## Purpose

The recovery and joy pillar. L6 prevents the OS from becoming a performance machine at the expense of lived experience. Hobbies restore energy, provide identity outside of work, and generate the positive affect that Shawn Achor's research shows precedes high performance. L6 is not a reward — it is maintenance.

**Core principle (L6):** Joy is not earned after work is done. It is what makes the work sustainable.

- **Shawn Achor (Happiness Advantage):** L6 activities are integrated into the Habit Tracker as positive psychology inputs. Celebrating life actively is part of the performance system, not separate from it.
