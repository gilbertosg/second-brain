## Physical Mastery Triangle

Robbins defines physical vitality as the intersection of three systems. All three must function to generate **Vitality, Energy, and Power.**

```
         BIOCHEMICAL
        (What you put in)
            /\
           /  \
          /    \
         /      \
STRUCTURAL ---- PSYCHOLOGY
(How you move)  (What you think/feel)
```

| Domain | Definition | Gil's Current State |
|---|---|---|
| **Biochemical** | Nutrition, supplementation, hydration, toxin load | MCH elevated (B12/folate likely deficient); homocysteine 7.55 (above optimal); supplementation gap since Lifeforce program |
| **Structural** | Biomechanics, posture, exercise form, injury status | Left knee (2018 lateral meniscus surgery); requires form-conscious exercise selection |
| **Psychology** | Emotional baseline, stress chemistry, mindset | High-Beta survival mode dominant (TPM stress loop); addressed via Dispenza protocol |

**Deficiency in any one vertex degrades the entire triangle.** Gil's primary constraint is Biochemical (B12, hormone deficiency) combined with Psychology (cortisol dysregulation). The structural domain is managed but requires ongoing attention to the left knee.

## The Cycle of Imbalance vs. the Cycle of Balance

### Cycle of Imbalance (Current State Risk)

```
Poor nutrition / toxic load
        ↓
Biochemical deficiency (B12, testosterone, DHEA)
        ↓
Structural weakness (inflammation, muscle loss)
        ↓
Psychological stress (anxiety, low motivation)
        ↓
Poor decisions (skipped workouts, poor sleep, poor nutrition)
        ↓
Deeper deficiency → loop accelerates
```

**Gil's specific imbalance pattern:** Weight gain since 2023 → testosterone decline → reduced motivation for fitness → further weight gain → further testosterone suppression.

### Cycle of Balance (Target State)

```
Clean nutrition / supplementation
        ↓
Hormonal optimization (T, DHEA-S restored)
        ↓
Structural capacity (heavy lifts, Zone 2, left knee stable)
        ↓
Psychological resilience (Alpha/Theta baseline)
        ↓
Consistent execution → further improvement
        ↓
Virtuous cycle sustained
```

Breaking into the Cycle of Balance requires a simultaneous intervention across all three vertices, not a sequential one. Fixing biochemistry alone without addressing psychology sustains the stress-cortisol suppression loop.

## The 3 Mandates

### Mandate 1: Stop the Poisoning

Eliminate inputs that degrade biochemical balance.

**Key toxin sources identified:**

| Toxin Source | Mechanism | Gil Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Coffee** (pH 5.1) | Acid load; disrupts mineral absorption; cortisol spike | Reduce to 1 cup/day before noon; avoid on empty stomach |
| **Mercury-high fish** (tuna, swordfish, king mackerel) | Heavy metal accumulation; neurological and hormonal disruption | Replace with low-mercury options: salmon, sardines, tilapia |
| **Refined sugar** | Insulin spike → insulin resistance → metabolic dysfunction | Already tracked via OKR (eat well 4×/week) — quantify sugar specifically |
| **Alcohol** | Disrupts liver function, raises estrogen, suppresses testosterone | L1 OKR (≤ 3×/week) is a floor; reduction directly raises testosterone |
| **Dry cleaning chemicals (PERC)** | Endocrine disruptor; xenoestrogen | Air out dry-cleaned garments before wearing; store outside bedroom |
| **Tap water (unfiltered)** | Chlorine, fluoride, trace pharmaceuticals | Use filtered water; invest in under-sink or countertop filter |

### Mandate 2: Cleanse and Detoxify

The 7 Principles of Detoxification:

| Principle | Protocol | Gil Application |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Liver/Colon cleanse** | Periodic detox protocols; fiber intake | Increase cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, Brussels sprouts) to support liver Phase I/II detox |
| **2. Super-hydration** | Half body weight in ounces of water/day | At 93.5 kg (206 lb): 103 oz / ~3 liters minimum daily — add 500 mL during heavy lift sessions |
| **3. Lymphasize** | Rebounding (mini-trampoline) or vigorous movement activates lymphatic system | Walking Luna contributes; add rebounder sessions or jump rope |
| **4. Skin brushing** | Dry brush before shower; removes dead skin cells; activates lymphatic drainage | 2 minutes pre-shower, 3–4×/week |
| **5. Breathing** | Deep diaphragmatic breathing removes acidic CO2 waste | Priming protocol (Life Force Step 7) covers this; add box breathing during work breaks |
| **6. Oil pulling / oral hygiene** | Reduces systemic bacterial load via mouth | Optional; low priority vs. other interventions |
| **7. Green foods** | Chlorophyll-rich foods alkalize the body | Daily greens powder or 1 cup leafy greens per meal |
