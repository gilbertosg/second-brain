## Critical Health Alerts (Updated May 2026)

### Alert 1: hs-CRP Escalation (NEW — May 2026)
**hs-CRP: 1.2 mg/L** (Function Health, May 19, 2026) — up from 0.49 mg/L in Jan 2026. A 2.4x increase in 4 months. Systemic inflammation is rising. Five out-of-range biomarkers from the Function Health panel form a unified metabolic risk signal: elevated hs-CRP + low HDL Large + low LDL Peak Size + borderline glucose + low omega-3. All five converge on the same root cause: early insulin resistance / metabolic dysfunction driven by visceral fat, chronic stress, and omega-3 deficiency.

**Immediate action required:**
- Begin Omega-3 EPA/DHA 2–3g/day — addresses hs-CRP, HDL Large, and LDL Peak Size directly
- Begin Zone 2 cardio 4×/week — primary metabolic intervention
- Reduce refined carbohydrates and alcohol
- Retest hs-CRP in 8–12 weeks to confirm intervention response

### Alert 2: Free Testosterone (Critical — May 2026, First Measurement)
**Free Testosterone: 54.0 pg/mL** (Function Health, May 19, 2026) — the biologically active fraction of testosterone is at 36% of the Medicine 3.0 optimal minimum (148.8 pg/mL). The action trigger (< 150 pg/mL) is breached. This is the first time Free T has been directly measured. Prior panels measured only Total T, which includes the biologically inert SHBG-bound fraction — making the data misleading. Total T improved from 262 → 300 ng/dL (+14.5%), the first upward reversal since Aug 2023, but this improvement is largely offset by the Free T finding. DHEA-S remains at 215 ug/dL (53% below its 462 peak in Aug 2023). Low Free Testosterone directly threatens the L3 Deep Work KR and Principal Promotion timeline via fatigue, reduced cognitive focus, reduced motivation, and degraded muscle recovery.

**Intervention stack (four-book synthesis):**
- **Tier 1 (immediate, self-managed):** Restart DHEA + B-complex/Methylfolate/B12 + Magnesium glycinate + Zinc + Omega-3. Begin Dispenza morning protocol (HRV-tracked). Begin Zone 2 cardio 4×/week. Eliminate or minimize alcohol.
- **Tier 2 (Q2 2026, physician-directed):** Full hormone panel → evaluate HCG/Clomiphene/TRT. Schedule DEXA scan. Schedule VO2 Max test. Consider 30-day CGM.
