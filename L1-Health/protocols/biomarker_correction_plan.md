## Weekly Training Architecture

Training day selection is a biomarker correction prescription, not a preference. Hormone and inflammation biology require specific stimulus types on specific frequencies.

| Day | Session | Primary Lift | Duration | Primary Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Alpha — Horizontal Push/Pull + Anterior Chain | Barbell Back Squat (6–10 reps, 1–2 RIR) | 55 min | Free T — acute testosterone + GH pulse from Barbell Back Squat |
| **Tuesday** | Zone 2 Cardio | Indoor bike, 130–150 BPM steady-state | 45 min | hs-CRP — visceral fat oxidation; primary anti-inflammatory intervention |
| **Wednesday** | Beta — Vertical Push/Pull + Posterior Chain | Romanian Deadlift (8–12 reps, 1–2 RIR) | 55 min | Free T — posterior chain endocrine response; corrects hip flexor inhibition from desk work |
| **Thursday** | Zone 2 Cardio | Indoor bike, 130–150 BPM steady-state | 45 min | hs-CRP — second visceral fat session of the week |
| **Friday** | Gamma — Unilateral + Metabolic Stress | Bulgarian Split Squat (10–15 reps) | 50 min | Free T + hs-CRP — bilateral asymmetry correction; EPOC-driven fat oxidation |
| **Saturday** | Zone 5 — VO2 Max Intervals | 4 min max aerobic effort × 4 rounds, 4 min recovery | 40 min | Glucose + HbA1c — VO2 Max elevation drives non-insulin-mediated glucose uptake |
| **Sunday** | Rest + 120-min Meal Prep | — | 120 min meal prep | ALL — nutritional compliance for the entire week |

**Zone 2 minimum:** 4 sessions/week is non-negotiable for hs-CRP correction. If scheduling forces a conflict, Zone 2 takes priority over Zone 5 — move Zone 5 to a different day before dropping a Zone 2 session.

**Knee accommodation:** Alpha (Back Squat) and Beta (RDL) — manage depth and load for left knee (lateral meniscus fracture, 2018). Apply Fitbod load adjustments as needed. Never sacrifice knee health for progressive overload numbers.

## Daily Execution Schedule

### MORNING BLOCK — Every Day (6:00–7:00 AM)

#### 6:00 AM — Wake Protocol (10 min)

| Action | Duration | Biomarker Target | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wake — no phone for first 10 minutes | 10 min | Free T | Phone stimulation triggers cortisol spike → elevates SHBG → suppresses Free T bioavailability. Protect this window. |
| Step outside or stand by window for sunlight exposure | Concurrent with wake | Free T + Sleep | Morning photon input anchors circadian rhythm and supports testosterone pulsatility timing for the following sleep cycle. |

#### 6:10 AM — 10 Power Breaths (3 min)

**Protocol:** 1:4:2 diaphragmatic ratio — inhale 1 count, hold 4 counts, exhale 2 counts. Execute 3 sets of 10 repetitions.

**Biomarker target:** Free T + hs-CRP
**Mechanism:** Deep diaphragmatic breathing physically activates the parasympathetic nervous system via vagus nerve stimulation. This directly suppresses the sympathetic cortisol spike that:
1. Elevates SHBG (the molecule sequestering Free T at 54.0 pg/mL)
2. Activates NF-κB (the transcription factor driving the hs-CRP elevation)

This is the single lowest-cost, highest-mechanism-density action in the plan. 3 minutes. No equipment. Targets both critical deficiencies simultaneously.

#### 6:15 AM — Supplement Stack (5 min)

Take all morning supplements together with the Longevity Shake. The shake's MCT oil is the fat substrate required for optimal Omega-3 and D3 intestinal absorption.

| Supplement | Dose | Biomarker Target | Critical Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Creatine Monohydrate** | 5g in water | Free T (neuroprotection during sleep deprivation; cognitive ATP for Deep Work) | Take first — dissolve in water, drink while preparing shake |
| **Omega-3 EPA/DHA** | 2g (2,000 mg combined) | 🔴 hs-CRP (primary anti-inflammatory precursor); Omega-3 Total (deficit correction); HDL Large; LDL Peak Size | **Must be taken with fat-containing meal** — MCT oil in shake provides the bile trigger for intestinal absorption |
| **Vitamin D3 + K2 (MK-7)** | 5,000 IU D3 + 100 mcg K2 | Free T — D3 upregulates the 1,000+ genes including those governing testosterone synthesis | Do NOT take in evening — D3 is inversely correlated with melatonin production |

**Supplement timing is deterministic.** Evening Creatine or morning Magnesium L-Threonate will reduce efficacy. Follow the timing exactly.

#### 6:20 AM — Longevity Shake (Breakfast)

| Component | Dose | Biomarker Target |
|---|---|---|
| Whey isolate or organic plant protein | 30g | Free T — leucine threshold for mTOR; muscle preservation; 150–180g protein/day target requires front-loading |
| MCT oil | 1 tbsp | Glucose — rapid, non-glycemic cerebral fuel with no insulin response; also enables supplement absorption |
| Frozen blueberries | 1 handful | hs-CRP — anthocyanins and polyphenols downregulate inflammatory cytokine cascades |
| Lion's Mane / Cordyceps powder | 1 tsp | Free T — adaptogen support for the adrenal-endocrine axis; Cordyceps specifically supports Leydig cell testosterone production |
| Unsweetened macadamia nut milk | 10 oz | HbA1c — zero glycemic load |

**No refined sugar. No fruit juice. No cow's milk.** The shake is designed to provide protein and cognitive fuel with zero glycemic impact.

#### 6:40 AM — Exercise Block

Execute the day's session per the weekly training architecture above. Log every set in Fitbod — track weight, reps, and RIR notation.

**Progressive overload protocol (strength days):**
- If you complete the upper rep target (e.g., 10 reps on Back Squat) across all 3–4 sets with clean form and 1–2 RIR: add 2.5%–5% load in the next session for that lift.
- If you miss the upper rep target on any set: hold the load.
- Never add load if form degrades — form failure = CNS limit reached, not a cue to push harder.

### MIDDAY BLOCK — Every Workday (12:00–12:45 PM)

#### 12:00 PM — Lunch (Lean Modular Bowl from Sunday Batch Prep)

Pull the pre-portioned glass container from the refrigerator. No decision required.

| Component | Dose | Biomarker Target |
|---|---|---|
| Batch-cooked turkey breast or chicken | 6–7 oz | Free T — protein for muscle preservation; zinc in chicken supports testosterone synthesis |
| Roasted sweet potato or quinoa | 1 cup | Glucose — complex carbohydrates are sequenced HERE (midday), never at dinner; this is the primary carb window |
| Roasted broccoli/cauliflower | 2 cups | hs-CRP — sulforaphane in cruciferous vegetables directly suppresses NF-κB; volume satiety without caloric density |
| Avocado | ¼ | Free T + hs-CRP — monounsaturated fat (oleic acid) supports Leydig cell function; anti-inflammatory |

**No processed food. No office snacks. No refined carbohydrates.** The Nutritional Architecture Adherence metric (1/0) is 0 the moment the batch prep bowl is replaced with anything from a vending machine or restaurant.

#### 12:30 PM — Post-Prandial Walk (10 min, mandatory)

Step outside and walk at a moderate pace for 10 minutes immediately after finishing lunch.

**Biomarker target:** Glucose (100 mg/dL) + HbA1c (5.2%)

**Mechanism:** Contracting skeletal muscle uses a non-insulin-mediated glucose transporter (GLUT4) that activates independently of insulin signaling. A 10-minute walk after a meal reduces the acute post-prandial glucose excursion by 15–30%. At a fasting glucose of 100 mg/dL and HbA1c at the 5.2% ceiling, this 10-minute habit is the margin between staying within the optimal band and breaching the 5.3% action trigger.

This action costs 10 minutes and has no barrier to execution. It is non-negotiable.

#### 7:00 PM — Dinner (Omega/Recovery Plate)

| Component | Dose | Biomarker Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Wild-caught salmon** (3 nights/week minimum) OR grass-fed flank steak | 6–8 oz | Omega-3 Total + hs-CRP (salmon: EPA/DHA from food source amplifies supplement stack); Free T (zinc in steak for testosterone synthesis) |
| Spinach / arugula / mixed leafy greens | Large portion | hs-CRP (anti-oxidant polyphenols); Homocysteine (folate for methylation cycle — currently 7.55 umol/L elevated) |

| **Zero starchy carbohydrates** | — | HbA1c + Free T — absence of dietary carbohydrates at dinner drops blood glucose and insulin rapidly; low-insulin environment enables endogenous GH release during the first slow-wave sleep cycle → testosterone synthesis |

**Alcohol rule during correction phase:** Maximum 1–2 drinks per event, maximum 3 events per week. Preferred: zero alcohol on weekdays. Each alcohol event elevates SHBG and suppresses Free T for 24–48 hours — at 54.0 pg/mL, there is no buffer to absorb this loss.

#### 9:00 PM — Digital Sunset

**All screens off by 9:00 PM.**

**Biomarker target:** Free T
**Mechanism:** Blue light from screens after 9 PM suppresses melatonin secretion and delays sleep onset by 30–90 minutes. Each hour of delayed sleep onset reduces testosterone pulsatile amplitude during that sleep cycle. Free T restoration depends on consistent deep sleep architecture. The Digital Sunset is not a wellness preference — it is a biomarker intervention.

Use this window (9:00–10:30 PM) for: reading (physical book), conversation with Luisa, or passive recovery (stretching).

#### 9:30 PM — Magnesium L-Threonate (30–60 min before sleep target)

| Supplement | Dose | Biomarker Target | Critical Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Magnesium L-Threonate** | ~145 mg elemental Mg (check brand label; typically 1,000–1,500 mg raw compound) | 🔴 Free T (primary sleep architecture intervention) + hs-CRP (overnight cellular inflammation resolution) | **L-Threonate only** — not Glycinate, Citrate, or Oxide. Only L-Threonate crosses the blood-brain barrier. Suppresses excitatory neurotransmitters; binds GABA receptors; increases slow-wave (Deep) and REM sleep duration — the windows where testosterone is synthesized and where inflammatory cytokines are cleared. |

#### 10:30 PM — Sleep

Lights out by 10:30 PM. Wake at 6:00 AM = 7.5 hours in bed (accounting for sleep latency: ~7:20 hr actual sleep).

**Biomarker target:** Free T (CRITICAL)
**Mechanism:** Testosterone is synthesized in pulses during sleep — primarily during slow-wave and REM stages. At 54.0 pg/mL Free T, Gil cannot afford to compress sleep. Every night below 7 hours measurably reduces morning Free T levels. This is not a suggestion — it is a production schedule for the primary missing hormone.

## Sunday Schedule (Rest + Meal Prep)

Sunday is the highest-leverage day of the week. The 120-minute batch prep determines nutritional compliance for all 7 days.

| Time | Action | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6:00–6:40 AM | Morning routine (sunlight, 10 Power Breaths, supplements, Longevity Shake) | 40 min | Same as weekdays |
| 7:00–8:00 AM | Rest — no structured exercise (CNS recovery from Saturday Zone 5) | 60 min | Free T recovery: CNS needs one full rest day |
| 10:00 AM | **120-Minute Sunday Meal Prep** | 120 min | See checklist below |

### Sunday Batch Prep Checklist

**Proteins:**
- [ ] 1.5 kg chicken breast OR turkey tenderloins — season with salt, pepper, turmeric (anti-inflammatory curcuminoids); roast Tray A at 400°F for 25–35 min
- [ ] 3 portions wild-caught salmon (6–8 oz each) for Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday dinners — store raw in refrigerator

**Vegetables:**
- [ ] Broccoli + asparagus + Brussels sprouts — lightly coat in EVOO; roast Tray B at 400°F for 20–25 min
- [ ] Leafy greens (spinach/arugula) — wash and store dry for dinner salads

**Carbohydrates:**
- [ ] 2 cups quinoa OR brown rice — boil; spread flat to cool rapidly (cooling increases resistant starch content, feeds microbiome, blunts glycemic response); portion 1 cup per lunch container

- [ ] Portion into glass containers (mandatory — no plastic): 6–7 oz protein + 1 cup complex carb + 2 cups vegetables
- [ ] Label: Mon/Tue/Wed containers → refrigerator; Thu/Fri containers → freezer (move to fridge Thursday morning)

**Shake prep:**
- [ ] Pre-portion 5 bags: 30g protein powder + 1 handful frozen blueberries + 1 tsp mushroom powder per bag
- [ ] Store macadamia nut milk in refrigerator
- [ ] Confirm MCT oil, Creatine, Omega-3, D3/K2, Mg L-Threonate supplies are sufficient for the week

## Physician Consult Preparation (Schedule: Q2 2026, Urgent)

The Free T action trigger is breached on Panel 1. One confirmed panel = 1 of the 2 required for pharmacological intervention eligibility.

**Lab panel to request at the appointment:**

| Lab | Why |
|---|---|
| Free Testosterone (fractionated) | Baseline confirmation; track against 54.0 pg/mL |
| Total Testosterone | For SHBG-adjusted comparison |
| SHBG (Sex Hormone Binding Globulin) | Determines whether the problem is bioavailability (SHBG high) or production (LH/FSH disrupted) |
| LH (Luteinizing Hormone) | If low: central (HPG axis not signaling properly); if high: primary testicular failure |
| FSH (Follicle Stimulating Hormone) | Confirms LH interpretation |
| Estradiol (E2) | DHEA-S can aromatize to estrogen; excess E2 further suppresses Free T |
| DHEA-S (retest) | Baseline was 215 ug/dL — 53% below 2023 peak; drives upstream testosterone production |
| Cortisol (AM draw) | Baseline was 7.6 ug/dL (low); low cortisol + low T = adrenal axis evaluation needed |

**Decision tree to discuss with physician:**

```
SHBG result?
  ├── SHBG HIGH (> 35 nmol/L) → bioavailability problem
  │   └── LH result?
  │       ├── LH low → central HPG suppression → Clomiphene or Enclomiphene indicated
  │       └── LH normal → SHBG-specific interventions (lifestyle; consider low-dose TRT)
  └── SHBG NORMAL → production problem
      └── LH/FSH result?
          ├── LH/FSH low → pituitary/hypothalamic dysfunction → Clomiphene indicated
          └── LH/FSH high → primary testicular insufficiency → TRT indicated
```
