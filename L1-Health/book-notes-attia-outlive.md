## Core Paradigm: Medicine 3.0

| Framework | Approach | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Medicine 2.0** | Treat disease after diagnosis | Lifespan (chronological survival) |
| **Medicine 3.0** | Prevent disease decades before symptoms | Lifespan + Healthspan (cognitive, physical, emotional quality) |

Medicine 3.0 treats the body as a system to be optimized proactively, not repaired reactively. As a TPM, Gil already applies this framework to aerospace programs — the same logic applies to biology.

## The Four Horsemen

The four disease categories causing the majority of adult deaths after age 50. Aggressive early screening delays or prevents each.

1. **Metabolic Dysfunction (Type 2 Diabetes):** Insulin resistance is the root accelerant. It fuels the other three horsemen. Gil's current ApoB (62) and triglycerides (44) are excellent; the weight OKR (−8 kg) directly reduces metabolic risk.
2. **Cardiovascular Disease:** Driven by apolipoprotein B (ApoB) penetrating the arterial wall. Gil's ApoB of 62 is strong. Attia recommends driving ApoB as low as possible, as early as possible. **No current action needed — maintain and monitor.**
3. **Cancer:** Genomic instability. Primary defense: metabolic health optimization and early detection via liquid biopsies. Exercise reduces cancer mortality by ~40%.
4. **Neurodegenerative Disease:** Strongly correlated with metabolic dysfunction and physical inactivity. Gil's low testosterone (262 ng/dL) and weight gain are risk factors for accelerated cognitive aging.

## The Four Exercise Pillars

### 1. Zone 2 Cardio
- **Definition:** Steady-state aerobic exercise at conversational pace (elevated but able to speak in full sentences).
- **Protocol:** 45–60 minutes, 3–4 sessions/week.
- **Mechanism:** Builds mitochondrial density and efficiency; the metabolic engine for all other energy demands.
- **Gil application:** Florida heat requires indoor solution. Wahoo or Peloton smart bike at Cirrus Drive apartment. Execute four 45-minute sessions per week.

### 2. VO2 Max Training
- **Definition:** Peak oxygen uptake under maximum aerobic effort. The single strongest predictor of longevity in the exercise literature.
- **Protocol:** 4×4 intervals — 4 minutes maximum effort, 4 minutes active recovery, repeat 4 times.
- **Frequency:** 1 session/week.
- **Target (age 34 male):** ≥ 50 mL/kg/min (good); ≥ 55 mL/kg/min (elite). Gil's current baseline is unmeasured — schedule VO2 Max test.
- **Gil application:** One dedicated interval session per week. Track VO2 Max score annually via sports science facility (Orlando or Cocoa area).

### 3. Strength Training
- **Definition:** Heavy resistance training to combat sarcopenia (age-related muscle loss) and improve glucose disposal.
- **Core lifts:** Deadlift, squat, farmer's carry, pull-ups.
- **Protein target:** 1.6–2.2 g/kg body weight. At 93.5 kg current weight: **150–206 g/day minimum.** Target 180 g/day.
- **Gil application:** 3 heavy sessions/week (already in L1 OKR). Left knee note: Romanian deadlift and trap bar deadlift reduce shear force vs. conventional deadlift.
- **⚠ Intersection:** Low testosterone (262 ng/dL) impairs muscle protein synthesis. Strength gains will be suboptimal until testosterone is addressed.

### 4. Stability
- **Definition:** Targeted movements to safely transfer force through the body and prevent falls in old age.
- **Why it matters:** Falls are a leading cause of death and disability in adults over 65. Stability training now is an investment in independence at age 80+.
- **Exercises:** Hip hinge mechanics, single-leg balance, anti-rotation core work, shoulder stability drills.
- **Gil application:** Incorporate stability work into warm-up or cool-down of each lifting session. Protects the left knee surgical site.

## Nutrition

Attia discards specific diet labels. Focus on two variables:

1. **Caloric control:** Maintain a mild deficit for body composition goals.
2. **Protein maximization:** 1.6–2.2 g/kg body weight. At target weight (85.5 kg): ~137–188 g/day.

**Current gap:** Gil's current protein intake is untracked. Begin tracking immediately. A CGM (continuous glucose monitor) for 30 days maps personal glycemic responses to specific foods and stress events.

## Sleep

- Chronic sleep deprivation is an endocrine disruptor. It accelerates cognitive decline and drives insulin resistance.
- **Environmental controls:** 65–68°F bedroom temperature. Absolute blackout curtains.
- **Tracking:** Oura Ring or Apple Watch Ultra — monitor Deep Sleep and REM cycles.
- **Alcohol elimination:** Alcohol disrupts REM sleep and raises resting heart rate. The L1 OKR (≤ 3 alcohol events/week) is a minimum floor; Attia's data argues for elimination.
- **⚠ Intersection with Testosterone:** Poor sleep directly suppresses testosterone production. 7:20 hr/night (L1 OKR) is the minimum. Optimizing sleep quality is the lowest-cost intervention for the testosterone crisis.
