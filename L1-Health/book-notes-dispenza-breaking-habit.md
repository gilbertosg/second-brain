# Book Notes — Breaking the Habit of Being Yourself

**Author:** Dr. Joe Dispenza
**Applied to:** Gilberto Silva Gonzalez, Sr. TPM at Blue Origin, age 34, Cocoa FL

---

## System Architecture: The Thought-Feeling Loop

The brain processes ~70,000 thoughts per day. 90% duplicate thoughts from the previous day. This creates a self-sustaining loop: thought → biochemical peptide release → feeling → new thought matching that feeling. Over time the body *memorizes* the emotion, which becomes the subconscious operating system.

**Gil's baseline assessment:** Years at Amazon and now Blue Origin have hardwired a neural architecture optimized for threat detection (project risks), deadline management, and relentless execution. The body has memorized high-alert, high-cortisol states. When attempting to rest in Cocoa, the nervous system may generate anxiety simply to maintain familiar cortisol levels.

This explains why the **cortisol reading of 7.6 ug/dL (below range)** is paradoxical — chronic cortisol dysregulation from sustained stress can deplete adrenal output, not just elevate it. The body's stress axis has been overworked.

---

## Neuroplasticity: Hebb's Law

> "Neurons that fire together, wire together."

Repeated thoughts and actions solidify neural networks. Pruning requires consciously firing new networks while stopping activation of old ones. Breaking a habit requires *stopping* the old neural pattern first, then *building* the replacement.

## Two Operating States

| State | Driver | Brainwave | Characteristic | TPM Context |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Survival Mode** | Sympathetic nervous system | High-Beta | Narrow focus; body/time-constrained; energy-draining | Default mode during Blue Origin work |
| **Creation Mode** | Parasympathetic nervous system | Alpha / Theta | Expanded focus; loses track of time; enables neuroplastic change | Required for meditation; rare during workdays |

True neuroplastic reprogramming only occurs in Creation Mode (Alpha/Theta). The standard TPM workday locks Gil in Survival Mode. Without deliberate intervention, the stress chemical loop self-reinforces indefinitely.

## The Quantum Model (Engineering Translation)

| Dispenza Concept | Engineering Equivalent |
|---|---|
| Quantum field | Cloud infrastructure — all possible states exist simultaneously |
| Physical reality | Local storage / hardware execution |
| Collapsing the wave function | Observer selecting a specific data state from distributed possibilities |

**Key principle:** Coherence between thought (intention) and feeling (emotion) determines what reality is pulled from the field. Broadcasting an intention of "successful program launch" while feeling anxiety creates a mismatched signal — the body executes the dominant emotion, not the stated intention.

**Practical translation:** Before entering a critical design review or milestone meeting, deliberately generate the emotional state of the desired outcome (calm authority, confidence) rather than waiting for the event to produce that feeling.

## The 28-Day Reprogramming Protocol

### Sprint 1 — Induction Mastery (Days 1–7)
**Objective:** Master Beta → Alpha brainwave transition.

**Technique:** "Body Part Space" induction. Focus sequentially on the physical volume of each body part (head, neck, torso, limbs), then expand attention to the volume of the room.

**Science:** Shifting focus from specific physical objects to open space forces the brain out of High-Beta analytical mode and into relaxed Alpha.

**Success metric:** Complete physical stillness; loss of time awareness.

**Gil execution:** Dedicated meditation corner in Cirrus apartment (not the bed, not the work desk). Leave iPhone in another room. Execute 20 minutes immediately after waking, before checking any device.

---

### Sprint 2 — Audit & Declaration (Days 8–14)
**Objective:** Isolate the primary emotion driving the limiting loop.

**Action:**
1. 10-minute induction.
2. 10-minute recognition: identify the recurring negative state (likely: chronic low-grade anxiety of program management; need for absolute control).
3. Write every thought accompanying the emotion ("I don't have enough time," "this team is moving too slow").
4. Write every physical action triggered by the emotion (rapid phone checking, sharp speech, jaw clenching).
5. Declare the emotion aloud in the dedicated space: *"I am ready to release my need for absolute control."*

---

### Sprint 3 — Circuit Breaker (Days 15–21)
**Objective:** Dismantle old neural pathways.

**Action:**
1. Complete Induction + Recognition (15 min).
2. Surrender: mentally hand the emotion of control to the quantum field.
3. Observe: review the audit list — commit the specific triggers to memory.
4. Redirect: spend 5 minutes visualizing trigger scenarios at Blue Origin. See yourself interrupting the response with the mental command **"Change!"**

**Workday application:** When a program deadline shifts or a technical blocker fires the old stress response, intercept with "Change!" before the cortisol loop executes.

---

### Sprint 4 — New Self Architecture (Days 22–28)
**Objective:** Install new neural pathways; alter baseline emotional chemistry.

**Action:**
1. Execute Sprints 1–3 rapidly (10 min).
2. 20–30 min Creation & Rehearsal:
   - Ask: *"What is the greatest ideal of myself as a Sr. TPM at Blue Origin?"*
   - Visualize: walking into the facility, posture, tone of voice with engineering teams.
   - Combine clear intention (the thought) with elevated emotion (gratitude, power, calm authority) — **feel this state before it occurs in physical reality.**

**Mechanism:** The body cannot distinguish between a vividly imagined future event and a real one. Mental rehearsal installs new neural hardware through repetition, the same way physical practice builds motor patterns.

## Biometric Progress Tracking

**Heart Rate Variability (HRV)** is the primary measurable output of this protocol.

- Higher HRV = parasympathetic dominance = Creation Mode = protocol is working.
- Lower HRV = sympathetic dominance = Survival Mode = stress chemical loop is winning.
- Measure via Apple Watch or Oura Ring. Log weekly trend.

**⚠ Intersection with testosterone crisis:** Chronic sympathetic activation (low HRV, high cortisol) is a primary suppressor of testosterone production. Shifting toward parasympathetic dominance (high HRV) directly supports testosterone recovery. This protocol is not separate from the hormonal intervention — it is part of the same system.
