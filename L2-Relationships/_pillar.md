# L2 — Relationships

## Purpose

Sustains the emotional infrastructure of Gil OS. Marriage, family, and close friendships provide the psychological foundation that allows Driver pillars (L1, L3) to operate at full capacity without isolation or burnout. Neglecting L2 creates systemic failure across all pillars — it is the hidden multiplier.

**Core principle (L2):** Invest in people before they need to be repaired.
