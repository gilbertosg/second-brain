## Why it matters

This test is typically used to diagnose a urinary tract infection (UTI).

Healthy urine is sterile, meaning the absence of microbes like bacteria and yeast. When bacteria is present, it typically indicates an infection, especially if you experience symptoms such as pain after urination, lower or upper back pain, or blood in your urine.
