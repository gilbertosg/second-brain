## Why it matters

Hyaline casts are the only casts that can be present in healthy urine. If, however, there are too many hyaline casts in urine, it can result in slow urine flow, which can be a result of strenuous exercise, diuretic medications, severe vomiting, or fever. Large amounts of hyaline casts may indicate kidney damage due to decreased blood flow to the kidneys.
