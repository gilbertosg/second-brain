## Why it matters

This test detects if there is blood in the urine. The medical term for blood in the urine is hematuria. Although blood in the urine may be concerning, it may also be benign.

When urine is tinted red or light pink, it may be caused by laxatives, beets, rhubarb, berries, riboflavin (vitamin B2), and vitamin B12. Blood in the urine, however, usually looks different and should be investigated by a doctor.

However, follow-up is typically required to determine the cause, which could also include menstruation, an enlarged prostate, a bladder infection, an autoimmune disease of the kidney, a kidney stone, bladder or kidney cancer, heavy exercise, or blood thinning medication, such as aspirin or heparin.
