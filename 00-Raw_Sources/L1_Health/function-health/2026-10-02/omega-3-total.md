## Why it matters

This test looks at the total amount of omega-3s. You will also see a further breakdown of three different types of omega-3s in your other biomarkers: EPA, DHA, and DPA. The total percentage, which is indicated in the Omega-3 Total / OmegaCheck biomarker, gives a more complete picture of your omega-3 status and helps assess your long-term intake of these essential fats.

Omega-3 fatty acids are anti-inflammatory fats found in some nuts, seeds, oils, and fish. Omega-3 fatty acids not only “cool off” inflammation but also reduce the risk of inflammation starting in the first place. Maintaining healthy levels may support cardiovascular, brain, and immune health while helping to balance your overall omega-6 to omega-3 ratio—an important marker of systemic inflammation.
