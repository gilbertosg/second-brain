# Bilirubin - Urine

Healthy urine and livers won’t have bilirubin, but high bilirubin levels can mean a problem with your liver.

## Why it matters

The liver makes bile, which is essential for digesting food, especially fat. This bile contains bilirubin. Most bilirubin comes from the body's normal process of breaking down old red blood cells. A healthy liver can usually get rid of excess bilirubin, but when the liver isn’t working properly it can cause buildup of bilirubin.

Elevated bilirubin in the urine is often a sign of early liver disease.

## Summary

No bilirubin is a sign of healthy urine.

## Foods to eat

The liver benefits from a whole food, low glycemic, anti-inflammatory diet. Those types of foods can help prevent conditions, such as type 2 diabetes, that lead to liver damage. Eating high-fiber foods, including fruits, vegetables, nuts, seeds, beans, whole grains, and high-quality protein, is also beneficial.
