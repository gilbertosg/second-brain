## Why it matters

Unusual urine color can be a sign of disease or dehydration. The scale of light to dark gives a picture of how much water is being flushed through the system. You can visibly check on your hydration by looking at the color of your urine. Fluids dilute the yellow pigments in urine, so the more you drink, the clearer your urine.

As with the occult blood test, other things can impact the color of your urine, too: water intake, B vitamins, laxatives, beets, rhubarb, and other deeply tinted foods. Additionally, unusual muscle damage from injury or a muscle disease can cause elevated levels of myoglobin that turn urine a reddish color.
If your urine is consistently discolored, the color test can help screen for and/or monitor several diseases and conditions, such as kidney disorders or urinary tract infections (UTIs).
