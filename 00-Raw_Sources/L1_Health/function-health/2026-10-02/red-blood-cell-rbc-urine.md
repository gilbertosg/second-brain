## Why it matters

This test checks for red blood cells in the urine. If present, the condition is called hematuria.

Red blood cells in the urine can come from the kidney (where urine is made) or anywhere in the urinary tract:

- Ureters (the tubes that carry the urine from the kidneys to the bladder)
- Bladder (where urine is stored)
- Prostate (in men)
- Urethra (the tube through which urine exits the body)

If there are red blood cells in the blood, it may or may not be visible. Seeing blood in the urine can be frightening, however, most of the time, the presence of red blood cells is not life-threatening.

If blood is present, is important to investigate the cause of hematuria because, occasionally, it is caused by a serious condition.

## Summary

Your body is constantly building blood, so it’s good for you to help it! Consuming blood-building foods with iron, B9, and B12 will help maintain a healthy red blood count.
