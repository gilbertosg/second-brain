## Why it matters

IGF-1 is used to calculate Z Scores. IGF-1 and this Z Score reflect growth hormone activity and provide a valuable measure to evaluate growth hormone status, particularly in relation to age and sex. Z scores help determine if an individual's IGF-1 level is within the expected range for their population group.

The reference range for Z Score is -2 to +2, and is described as follows:

–Z Score of 0 indicates IGF-1 level is average.

–Z Score less than -2.0 indicates a low level of IGF-1 and may suggest growth hormone deficiency.

–Z Score greater than 2.0 indicates growth hormone excess. This suggests acromegaly or excessive treatment dosage of growth hormone.

–Z Score within the range of -2 to +2 generally indicates normal growth hormone status and makes acromegaly less likely. However, there are exceptions, such as individuals with small growth hormone-secreting tumors or those with growth hormone deficiency but normal IGF-1 levels. Additional testing may be necessary in these cases to confirm the diagnosis or assess treatment paths
