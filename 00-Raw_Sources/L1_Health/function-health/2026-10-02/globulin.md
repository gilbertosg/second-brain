## Why it matters

Globulins are a group of proteins present in your blood. The immune system produces them in your liver. Globulins are crucial for liver function, blood clotting, and fighting infections.

Total globulins may increase in some chronic inflammatory diseases (such as TB and syphilis), multiple myeloma, collagen disease, and rheumatoid arthritis. Decreased levels of globulins are seen in hepatic dysfunction, renal disease, and various neoplasms.
