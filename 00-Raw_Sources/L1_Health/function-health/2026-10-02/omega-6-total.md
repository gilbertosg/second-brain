## Why it matters

This test looks at the total amount of omega-6s in your blood. The total percentage, shown in the Omega-6 Total biomarker, reflects your overall intake of these fats and their potential impact on inflammatory balance. This value represents the combined levels of linoleic acid (LA) and arachidonic acid (AA), two key omega-6 fatty acids. For a more complete picture, refer to the individual LA and AA biomarkers.

Omega-6 fatty acids are essential fats found in many vegetable oils, nuts, seeds, and processed foods. Your body needs omega-6s for normal function—including immune response and cell signaling—but too much can promote inflammation, especially when not balanced by adequate omega-3 intake. Keeping omega-6 levels in check and in proper ratio to omega-3s is key to supporting metabolic, cardiovascular, and immune health.
