## Why it matters

The AA/EPA ratio measures the balance between arachidonic acid (AA), a pro-inflammatory omega-6 fatty acid, and eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA), an anti-inflammatory omega-3 fatty acid. In many modern diets, particularly in the United States, the AA/EPA ratio tends to be excessively high due to a combination of high intake of AA-rich foods like red and processed meats, high consumption of linoleic acid-rich foods such as refined oils and processed foods that can convert to AA, and low intake of omega-3-rich foods like fish. A higher ratio means the body may be more prone to fueling inflammation rather than resolving it, making this an important marker for understanding the body's inflammatory environment.

The AA/EPA ratio works closely with other markers of inflammation and metabolic health, including C-reactive protein (CRP) and interleukin-6 (IL-6). Maintaining a healthy balance between AA and EPA is important for controlling inflammation, supporting cardiovascular health, and regulating immune responses. Measuring the AA/EPA ratio provides valuable insight into whether the body is leaning toward a pro-inflammatory or anti-inflammatory state. For example, an elevated AA/EPA ratio may indicate an increased risk for chronic conditions such as heart disease, metabolic syndrome, or diabetes. Tracking this biomarker can also help evaluate the effects of dietary interventions, such as increasing omega-3 intake or reducing sources of excessive omega-6 fats.

- **High AA/EPA ratio:** Associated with chronic inflammation and a higher risk for chronic conditions such as cardiovascular disease and metabolic syndrome
- **In-range AA/EPA ratio:** Reflects a more anti-inflammatory state and may be protective against chronic inflammatory diseases, although extremely low levels could impair normal immune and healing responses.

Monitoring the AA/EPA ratio, especially alongside other inflammatory markers and clinical symptoms, allows for more personalized strategies to manage inflammation. Interventions may include increasing omega-3 intake through diet or supplementation, reducing excess omega-6 consumption, and supporting overall metabolic and immune health.

**Summary**

The AA/EPA ratio shows whether the body is tilted toward a state of inflammation or balance by measuring the relationship between arachidonic acid (AA), which promotes inflammation, and eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA), which helps reduce it. A root cause approach focuses on lowering excess AA, increasing EPA through diet and supplementation, and supporting overall metabolic health to help reduce inflammation at its source.

## Summary

An AA/EPA ratio in the normal range indicates a healthy balance between pro-inflammatory and anti-inflammatory fatty acids, suggesting that the body can respond to injury or infection without tipping into chronic inflammation. This balance supports heart health, brain function, immune resilience, and metabolic stability.

To maintain it, a root cause approach includes regularly consuming omega-3-rich foods like fatty fish and flaxseeds while limiting excess intake of AA-rich foods such as red and processed meats, as well as linoleic acid-rich foods like refined vegetable and seed oils and processed snacks that can convert to AA. Omega-3 supplements can reinforce anti-inflammatory pathways, while botanicals like curcumin may help modulate low-grade inflammation. Supportive therapies, such as regular exercise, stress management, and gut health support with probiotics and fiber-rich foods, enhance long-term stability. Periodic retesting can ensure the ratio stays in range and allow for early intervention if subtle shifts begin to occur.

## Foods to eat

EPA sources include wild or sustainably-raised coldwater fish (herring, mackerel, salmon, and sardines), healthy oils (flaxseed and walnut), nuts, seeds, and pasture-raised eggs.

AA is found in refined, hydrogenated fats and oils, solid fats (like butter), and plant-based products with hydrogenated oils (like alternative milks, yogurts, and cheeses), which are often used to create a silky and smooth texture.
