## Why it matters

Leukocyte esterase is an enzyme produced by white blood cells. It is measured in urine as an indirect marker of white blood cells, which are part of the immune response.

When leukocyte esterase is present in urine, it typically indicates that white blood cells have entered the urinary tract, often in response to inflammation or infection.
