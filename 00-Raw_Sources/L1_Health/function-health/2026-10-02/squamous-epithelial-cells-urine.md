## Why it matters

The squamous epithelial cells are composed of the type of epithelial cell that is most common to the skin, blood vessels, and lower part of the urethra or genital tract. Having a small number of epithelial cells in the urine is normal. A larger amount may suggest contamination from the genital tract, kidney disease, or another serious medical condition.
