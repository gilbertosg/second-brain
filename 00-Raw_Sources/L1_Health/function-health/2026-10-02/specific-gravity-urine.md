## Why it matters

Specific Gravity tests the concentration of particles in the urine and is a quick way for a physician to tell if the kidneys are trying to compensate for an abnormality.

**Under concentrated:** This may signal a rare condition called diabetes insipidus, which causes thirst and the excretion of large amounts of diluted urine.

**Too concentrated:** This is most commonly associated with dehydration. This may also be a sign of kidney dysfunction but should be correlated with creatinine in the blood.

Medicine interferences: Medications containing sucrose or dextran. Chinese medicines and herbs may also impact or interfere with testing.
