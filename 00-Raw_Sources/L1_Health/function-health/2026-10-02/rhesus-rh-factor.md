## Why it matters

Many individuals are unaware of their blood type. However, it is important to know both you and your partner's blood type when it comes to fertility, as it can determine whether there is a risk for Rh incompatibility. This occurs when the mother's blood is Rh-negative, and the baby's blood is Rh-positive, which can cause the mother's immune system to produce antibodies that attack the baby's blood, potentially leading to health complications.

If you discover that you are at risk for Rh incompatibility, it is recommended that you consult with your doctor regarding the administration of immune globulins, such as RhoGAM, which can counteract this incompatibility.
