# L3 — Work / Career / Mission

## Purpose

The primary professional pillar. Drives income, identity, and mission. L3 splits into two distinct tracks with different strategic priorities:
1. **Career track** (Blue Origin): Driver. Path to Principal TPM (L7).
2. **Business track** (E7 brand): Passenger. Saturday-morning restricted. Feeds L7-Contribute.

**Core principle (L3):** Do fewer things at higher quality. Depth over breadth.

## Strategic Context

### Driver Track: Principal TPM Promotion
The single most important career objective for 2026. The "path to Principal" KR requires a documented promotion plan agreed with management — not just performance, but explicit level progression. The **Agent Award** KR signals that AI/automation work is a differentiator for the promotion case. **Deep Work (10 hr/week)** is the non-negotiable input metric that makes this achievable while staying under 40 hr/week total.

### Passenger Track: E7 Brand
E7 (effectiveatlife.com) translates Gil OS productivity systems for US and Latin American markets. Work is restricted to **Saturday mornings only**. This constraint is not optional — if Blue Origin work pressure increases, E7 Saturday sessions are the first to pause. The goal is consistency over 52 weeks, not intensity in any given week.

- **Serenity Scheduling** — Product launch. Exact scope TBD; track in L3 project files.
- **TPM Samurai** — Brand or resource for TPM professionals. Track in L3 project files.
- **Personal CRM (40 contacts)** — Professional network management system.

## Key Methodologies Applied

- **Cal Newport (Deep Work):** 10 hr/week Deep Work target is the primary productivity KR. Calendar blocks for Deep Work set before meetings. Reject meetings that don't meet necessity threshold.
- **Cal Newport (Slow Productivity):** Work < 40 hr/week is a Slow Productivity KR — quality over quantity.
- **Demir & Carey Bentley (Winning the Week):** Friday Finish blocks Deep Work for the coming week before Monday starts.
- **James Clear (Atomic Habits):** Deep Work tracking on the Habit Tracker makes the 10 hr/week target a daily identity signal, not a weekly hope.
